Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how LokalSterne (“we”, “us”) processes personal data when you visit our website, contact us or use our services. We process personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable national data protection law.

2. Data we collect

When you visit our website, our hosting provider automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type, date and time of access and the pages you visit. This is necessary to deliver the website securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

When you use the contact form, we process your name, phone number, email address and message in order to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

When you use our services, we process the account, billing and usage data required to provide the services you have ordered (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

3. Customer feedback

Businesses use LokalSterne to collect feedback from their customers. For this feedback, the business is the controller and we process the data on its behalf under a data processing agreement. Customers who submit feedback should contact the relevant business to exercise their rights.

4. Sharing of data

We only share personal data with service providers that help us run our website and services, such as hosting, email and payment providers. They are bound by data processing agreements and may only use the data on our instructions. We do not sell personal data.

5. Storage period

We keep personal data only as long as it is needed for the purposes described above or as required by statutory retention obligations. Contact requests are deleted once they have been fully handled, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

6. Cookies

Our website only uses technically necessary cookies and similar technologies. If we introduce analytics or marketing tools in the future, we will ask for your consent beforehand.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing and to data portability. Where processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. Contact

To exercise your rights or if you have any questions about data protection, please use the contact form.
